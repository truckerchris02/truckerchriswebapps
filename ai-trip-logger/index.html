Your pay rates

Enter your own company's numbers. Everything stays on this device, in this browser — nobody else sees it. Every field here is optional too: fill in only the rates your company pays, and leave the rest blank. Pay lines in the app simply show what can be figured from whatever you've entered — blank rates count as $0 and never stop you from saving a trip.

Mileage

Mileage pay is figured from each trip's GPS / shift miles — never from route miles. On each trip you can enter how many of those GPS miles ran at night and how many ran on the weekend, and the bonuses are added for those miles.

Activity pay — $ each

Detention

Detention minutes on a stop are your total wait at that store. Only the minutes past the free minutes count toward detention pay, and the detention pay line only appears on a trip once you've entered a rate here.

Back up & restore

Your trips live in this browser on this device only. Export a backup regularly — if you clear your browser data or change phones without a backup, the log is gone. A weekly save is a good habit.

Restore from a JSON backup

Restoring replaces the trips and settings currently in the app with the ones in the backup file.

Start over

Erase every trip in the app — for example, after you've printed a pay period and want to begin the next one fresh. Export a backup first. Settings are kept.
