🚛 Rig Nav

Truck-only navigation built on HERE truck routing.

Before you drive
  • Always obey posted signs, road restrictions, and law enforcement. Signs override this app.
  • Verify bridge clearances, weight limits, and hazmat rules yourself. Map data can be wrong or out of date.
  • Routes depend on the truck profile you enter. A wrong height or weight means a wrong route.
  • This is not a replacement for a certified commercial truck GPS or your company's routing.
  • Do not type or tap the screen while the truck is moving.
